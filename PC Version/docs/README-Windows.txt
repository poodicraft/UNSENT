UNSENT — Chapter One: The Long Dusk  (PC version 1.0.1)
=========================================================

Some letters were never sent. Some haven't been written yet.

You are the new night clerk at the Office of Unsent Letters in Hushwater,
a seaside town where the sun has been setting for seventeen years. Read the
letters closely, explore the town, question its people, and decide who each
letter is really for.


HOW TO PLAY
-----------
Double-click UNSENT.exe. Nothing needs to be installed.

The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" (the game isn't
code-signed). Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

The game starts in fullscreen. Press F11 (or Alt+Enter) to switch between
fullscreen and a window. Saves and settings are kept in:
    %APPDATA%\UNSENT


CONTROLS
--------
  Left click .................. walk, talk to people, examine things
  A / D  or  Left / Right ..... walk   (W / S or Up / Down: step nearer / further)
  E ........................... talk to / examine whatever is nearest
  Space, Enter or click ....... continue the conversation
  1 - 9  or  Up / Down + Enter  choose an answer
  Tab  or  I .................. open / close the satchel (letters, clues, people, items)
  T ........................... turn a letter over (in the letter reader)
  Mouse wheel ................. scroll letters and lists
  Esc ......................... back / close a panel / pause menu
  F11  or  Alt+Enter .......... fullscreen / windowed

Hover the mouse over something to see what it is. Sparkles mark things worth
a closer look.

The loop: collect a letter from the Chute, read it (click the underlined
words; turn the envelope over), explore and ask people about your clues, and
when you have at least two good reasons, choose "Deliver a letter..." while
talking to someone.


SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
-------------------
  Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  Any graphics card with OpenGL 3.3 (or Direct3D 11)
  About 200 MB of free memory
